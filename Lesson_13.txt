LITHUANIAN — LESSON 13
Lesson 13 — Ar jūs esate…? Taip.
Voices: NARRATOR = English narrator · MALE = male native Lithuanian · FEMALE = female native Lithuanian
[PAUSE n] = n seconds of silence for the learner to answer or repeat. [SECTION: …] lines are not spoken.
Estimated running time: 30 min 18 s

[SECTION: Introduction]
NARRATOR: This is Lesson 13 of your Lithuanian course. Listen to this conversation. A man is meeting a woman he has been expecting.

FEMALE: Aš esu studentė.
[PAUSE 4]

FEMALE: tė
[PAUSE 3]
FEMALE: dentė
[PAUSE 3]
FEMALE: studentė
[PAUSE 3]
NARRATOR: A man is a "studentas." A woman is a "studentė." Remember "rytas," "vakaras," "vardas"? Masculine words often end in -as. Many feminine words end in -ė. Listen to both.
MALE: studentas
FEMALE: studentė
[PAUSE 2]

[PAUSE 5]
MALE: Aš esu studentas.
[PAUSE 4]
NARRATOR: How would a woman say "I'm a student"? Say it in her place.
[PAUSE 5]
FEMALE: Aš esu studentė.
[PAUSE 4]
NARRATOR: When you talk to someone, the word matches the person you're talking to. Listen to the man ask a woman, "Are you a student?"
MALE: Ar jūs esate studentė?
