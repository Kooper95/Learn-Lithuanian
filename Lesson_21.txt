[SECTION: Your own name]
NARRATOR: What about your own name? If it's an English name, Lithuanians often leave it as it is. But if it ends in a consonant, they may add "-as" to make it Lithuanian, and then call you with "-ai." For example, "Tom" becomes "Tomas," and you'd hear "Tomai!" Listen to how a Lithuanian friend might call an Australian named Mark.
FEMALE: Markai!
[PAUSE 2]
NARRATOR: Don't worry about this for your own name yet. Just recognise it when you hear it.
