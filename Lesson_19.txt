LITHUANIAN — LESSON 19
Lesson 19 — Pokalbis! (Conversation practice: chapter 1)
Voices: NARRATOR = English narrator · MALE = male native Lithuanian · FEMALE = female native Lithuanian
[PAUSE n] = n seconds of silence for the learner to answer or repeat. [SECTION: …] lines are not spoken.
Estimated running time: 27 min 49 s

[SECTION: Introduction]
NARRATOR: This is Lesson 19 of your Lithuanian course. Today there are no new words at all. Instead, you'll use everything from chapter 1 in new ways: listening in on conversations, correcting mistakes, solving a small mystery, and talking about yourself. Some answers have no single right version. When that happens, say whatever you can, then listen to one possible answer.
