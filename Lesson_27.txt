LITHUANIAN — LESSON 27
Lesson 27 — Pokalbis! (conversation practice: chapters 1 and 2)
Voices: NARRATOR = English narrator · MALE = male native Lithuanian · FEMALE = female native Lithuanian
[PAUSE n] = n seconds of silence for the learner to answer or repeat. [SECTION: …] lines are not spoken.
Estimated running time: 28 min 04 s

[SECTION: Introduction]
NARRATOR: This is Lesson 27 of your Lithuanian course, a practice lesson with no new words. Today you'll untangle a puzzle about four people, catch mistakes, finish other people's sentences, interview a stranger, and talk about yourself for half a minute. When an answer is open, say what you can, then listen to one version.
